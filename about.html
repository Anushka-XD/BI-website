Who we are

About Bhartiya Industries: Nurturing Legacy, Crafting Excellence

Welcome to Bhartiya Industries, a name synonymous with tradition, excellence, and a commitment to providing the finest in the rice milling industry. Established by the visionary Shri Surender Kumar Gupta (Kaka) in 1965, our journey has been one of unwavering dedication, spanning decades of experience and innovation. With our Vice President, Prateek Gupta, steering us towards the future, Bhartiya Industries stands as a proud testament to the values of quality, integrity, and continuous improvement.

50+ Years Of Experience

Our Founder's Legacy

Shri Surender Kumar Gupta (Kaka) is not just a founder but a visionary who has shaped the course of Bhartiya Industries with his unparalleled knowledge and experience in the rice milling industry. Since 1965, Kaka has been a stalwart, guiding the company through the dynamic landscape of agriculture and ensuring that each grain that leaves our facilities is a testament to his legacy of uncompromising quality.

Leadership for the Future

Prateek Gupta, our Vice President, brings a fresh perspective and dynamic leadership. With a deep understanding of present challenges and future opportunities, he upholds founder values while steering the company towards new heights. His strategic vision and commitment to innovation drive continuous growth.

Machinery and Production Capacity

Our plant is equipped with Bhuller and Stake machines for precision milling. Packing capacity reaches a remarkable 250 tons of rice daily. Paddy-to-rice conversion stands at an impressive 100 tons daily — efficiency and scale in every shift.

Technological Advancements

With 2 Sortex plants and a complete online paddy-to-rice facility, every grain undergoes thorough sorting and quality checks — guaranteeing the highest standards and a forward-thinking approach to milling.

Machinery Inventory

Our machinery inventory includes 2 Bhullar Z4 machines, each a testament to our dedication to precision and efficiency. In addition, we house a Stake 5001 and a Bhullar A5, showcasing our versatility and capability to handle various rice varieties and milling processes. These machines are not just tools; they are the embodiment of our commitment to delivering excellence at every stage of production.

Quality Assurance

Quality is the cornerstone of Bhartiya Industries. We adhere to stringent quality assurance protocols, ensuring that each grain of rice that leaves our facility meets the highest industry standards. Our commitment to quality extends beyond the processing floor to every facet of our operations, from sourcing the finest paddy to the final packaging of the premium rice varieties we offer.

Sustainability Initiatives

As custodians of the land and stewards of nature, Bhartiya Industries is deeply committed to sustainable and eco-friendly practices. We understand the importance of responsible agriculture and actively participate in initiatives that promote environmental consciousness. Our commitment to sustainability extends to water management, energy efficiency, and community development, ensuring that our operations leave a positive impact on both the environment and society.

Community Engagement

Bhartiya Industries is not just a business; it's a part of a larger community. We believe in fostering strong ties with the communities in which we operate. Through employment opportunities, skill development programs, and support for local initiatives, we aim to contribute to the socio-economic development of the regions we serve.

Global Outreach

While firmly rooted in the Indian soil, Bhartiya Industries has a global outlook. Our commitment to quality has garnered recognition beyond borders, and we proudly export our premium rice varieties to international markets. This global outreach not only expands our horizons but also serves as a testament to the universal appeal of the excellence we deliver.

Future Endeavours

As we navigate the ever-evolving landscape of the rice milling industry, Bhartiya Industries remains committed to innovation, sustainability, and customer satisfaction. Our journey is a testament to the dedication of our founder, the leadership of our Vice President, and the hard work of every individual in the Bhartiya Industries family. With a rich legacy behind us and a future full of promise, we invite you to join us on this journey of crafting excellence in every grain.

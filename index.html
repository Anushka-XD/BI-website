Sharbati Rice

Often referred to as the “queen of fragrance,” Sharbati is a special medium-grain variety from the fertile plains of India — cherished for its aromatic nature, tender texture, and delightful taste.

Aromatic Medium grain Nutrient-rich
  • Aroma that Captivates — natural fragrance that elevates every dish.
  • Tender Texture — grains stay separate and fluffy when cooked; ideal for biryanis and pilafs.
  • Nutrient-Rich Goodness — a good source of carbohydrates, vitamins, and minerals.
  • Quality Assured — rigorous testing from cultivation to processing, ethically sourced.

Try it in vegetable biryani, jeera rice, or creamy kheer — every grain delivers a sensory culinary journey.

Basmati Farming

Known for its distinct aroma, long grains, and fluffy texture, Basmati has been a culinary treasure for centuries. Cultivated primarily in the fertile regions of the Indian subcontinent, its allure lies in unique fragrance and delicate flavour.

Long grain Organic practices Sun-dried
  • Ideal Growing Conditions — fertile alluvial soil, sunlight, and balanced water in the Basmati belt.
  • Seed Selection — high-quality certified seeds preserving genetic integrity.
  • Water Management — careful irrigation for long, slender, aromatic grains.
  • Organic & Traditional — organic alternatives over synthetic chemicals; traditional sun-drying after harvest.
  • Milling Excellence — state-of-the-art milling with Sortex sorting for purity and cleanliness.

PR11 Rice

Also known as Pusa Basmati 1121, PR11 is a long-grain variety developed by the Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI). Cross-bred from Basmati and traditional rice, it offers a slender, aromatic grain prized for exceptional taste and texture.

Up to 8.2 mm Non-sticky Basmati blend
  • Physical Attributes — long, slender grains with a delicate non-sticky texture and Basmati-like aroma.
  • Cultivation — ideal soil and irrigation, with organic methods that minimise synthetic inputs.
  • Varieties — Pure PR11 Rice for authentic flavour; PR11 Basmati Blend for aromatic notes of both worlds.
  • Health Benefits — essential carbohydrates, protein, and fibre for a wholesome diet.

Perfect for fluffy biryanis and aromatic pilafs — a culinary marvel in every pack.

Traditional Basmati

Often called the “Queen of Fragrance,” Traditional Basmati stands as a symbol of culinary excellence. Cultivated for centuries in the Himalayan foothills, unique geography and climate give it its distinct aroma and long grains.

GI tagged Organic Gluten-free
  • Exceptional Aroma — sweet, nutty fragrance released during cooking.
  • Long Grain Structure — elongates further when cooked into fluffy, separate grains.
  • Delicate Flavour — complements dishes without overpowering other ingredients.
  • GI Tagged Farms — sourced from Geographical Indication tagged regions for authenticity.
  • Organic Farming — natural methods without harmful pesticides and chemicals.

Ideal for biryani bliss, pulao perfection, and everyday side dishes that deserve a touch of sophistication.
